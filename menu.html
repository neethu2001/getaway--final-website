Chef's Note

A Celebration of Flavours

At Coastal Flavour Restaurant, we celebrate the joy of flavours from every corner of the world.

Be it traditional Kerala coastal cuisine, North Indian delicacies, Oriental favourites, or global classics — our culinary team takes pride in creating every dish with passion and perfection.

If you crave a special dish or a taste from your home country, please feel free to speak with our Chef. We’ll be delighted to craft it especially for you just the way you like it.
