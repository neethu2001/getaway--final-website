Welcome to Getaway

A Boutique Coastal Retreat Awaits

Getaway Beach Resort, Kovalam is a boutique coastal retreat offering a refined seaside experience along the Arabian Sea.

With spacious sea-view accommodation, Coastal Flavour Restaurant, a Swimming Pool, Private Gazebo Dining and Outdoor event spaces, it is an inviting base to discover Kovalam.
