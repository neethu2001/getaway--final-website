Designed with families in mind, the Ocean View Two-Bedroom Family Suite offers an airy and comfortable environment where everyone can relax while still enjoying their own personal space.

The suite features two bedrooms and two bathrooms — one thoughtfully arranged for parents and another for children. Its spacious design makes it an ideal choice for families looking for comfort, convenience and privacy during their stay in Kovalam.

With beautiful ocean views and a peaceful resort atmosphere, the suite creates the perfect setting for a memorable family getaway.
